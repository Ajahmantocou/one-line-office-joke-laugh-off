One original one-line office joke (116 characters), written from scratch for make.fun's One-Line Office Joke Laugh-Off:

> Our office plant survived three managers, two restructures, and one merger — so HR asked it to mentor the new hires.

## Why this line

The joke avoids every office cliché — no coffee machine, no broken printer, no "per my last email", no meeting that should have been an email, no Monday. Instead it picks the one office resident nobody thinks of as staff: the plant in the corner.

It works in two beats. First, an escalation of corporate churn — three managers, two restructures, one merger — that the plant simply outlasts, quietly making it the most experienced thing in the building. Then the punchline flips the hierarchy: HR, the department that onboards people, asks the plant to mentor the new hires. The plant was never promoted, praised, or paid, yet it is now the mentor. That reversal is the laugh — the least powerful object in the office is the only one with institutional memory.

The wording is tight: every clause earns its place, the setup builds in threes, and the punchline lands in a single clause with no explanation needed. The em dash is the joke's only pause — the beat where the reader realises the plant is about to be promoted.
